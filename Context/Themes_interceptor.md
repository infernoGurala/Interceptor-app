The app uses a preset theme system. The user cannot define custom colors. This is intentional — to maintain a premium, consistent visual quality across all users.

Themes come in pairs — each theme has a light variant and a dark variant. The user picks a theme and then toggles between its light and dark version.

**Design language:**

- Extremely minimal. Options and controls are hidden beneath the surface and revealed only when needed.
- Every theme uses a single accent color. No multi-color palettes.
- The app should feel expensive and high-end. Think Linear, Nothing Phone UI, or Apple's premium aesthetic.
- Rounded surfaces, generous whitespace, subtle shadows or glows depending on the theme.
- No bright or clashing colors. No pride-flag style multi-color schemes.

**Preset theme examples (to be designed):**

- Obsidian — near-black base, cold white accent
- Ash — warm grey base, off-white accent
- Ink — deep navy base, soft cyan accent
- Paper — cream white base, charcoal accent
- Ember — dark base, muted amber accent

Theme selection is available in the Profile screen under settings. A live preview is shown before applying.
